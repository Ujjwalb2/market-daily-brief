Term of the dayFrom story 1

FII net selling

"F-I-I" · Foreign Institutional Investor

When foreign funds sell more Indian shares than they buy in a day, the gap is their net selling.

In today's news

FIIs sold ₹2,140 crore more than they bought yesterday: the fourth day in a row.

Opposite: net buying · Related: DII, FPI

Relation of the dayFrom Markets desk

Foreign selling ↔ a weaker rupee

FIIs sell Indian shares
↓ they swap rupees for dollars to take money home
More rupees for sale
↓ supply up, price down
Rupee weakens vs the dollar
↓ imports are priced in dollars
Gold, crude, electronics cost more
Seen today

FIIs sold ₹2,140 cr · USD-INR 0.1% · gold 0.4%

Learning of the dayFor SIP investors

Dips buy you more units

A fixed monthly SIP buys more units when prices fall and fewer when they rise. Over time your average cost ends up below the average price.
