Term of the dayFrom story 1

Short covering

When traders who bet on a fall buy back to close those bets, that buying itself pushes prices up. Most FII shorts have now been covered.

Relation of the dayFrom story 3

Cheaper crude, relief for India

Cause · Brent crude$117 → ~$93▼ ~20% from late-Oct peak
Oil import bill▼ smaller
Rupee & inflation▼ less pressure
Airlines, paints, tyres▲ cheaper inputs

India imports most of its oil, so every drop in crude ripples outward: fewer dollars spent, softer prices at home, lower costs for companies that burn or use oil.
